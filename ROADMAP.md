## Guardrails

- Runtime stays deterministic and fail-closed; no LLM/SLM, Android, STT/TTS or new crop expansion in this phase.
- Source URLs alone are not ingestion evidence. Published records require immutable snapshots, provenance, source scope and qualified human approval.
- Hashes verify integrity, not correctness. Agent agreement is not professional approval.
- Historical 660 behavioral simulations and any Linux vector smoke are not agronomic or Android evidence.
- Existing synthetic corn documents are historical/unvalidated; do not ingest or promote them.
- The 18 candidate records still have 0 retained source snapshots, 0 license decisions, 0 ready review packages, and 0 approvals.
- Do not equate AI reviewer disagreements with disagreements between agronomic sources.
